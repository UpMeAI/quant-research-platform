## SEEN by review and the final test (plan-coord ruling 11-23 (1), W05 semantics)

What the review read is development information (W05, plan-coord ruling 11-23, `W05_REVIEWED_IS_SEEN_RULING_V1`): every review fact pack delivered writes one SEEN `DATA_SEGMENT_MARK_V1` row into the trial ledger (`services/trial-ledger/review_pack_seen.js`, family `MARKET:<market>`, idempotent by the pack sha256, range = [earliest stated fact window start or the market data start, decision cutoff]); the lab's card step writes the mark of the pack it consumes (a refused mark is a refused card, `LAB_CARD_SEEN_MARK_<code>`), and the SEEN ranges of the lineage (`seen_ranges`) and the final test path are read from those ledger marks as of the run, not computed from the review agent's access log or the fact dates. They are SEEN for every candidate derived from the card. A candidate's final test segment must never overlap a SEEN range (V-2 `unseenClaim` with UNSEEN/SEEN marks from `seen.marksFor`, reused from the trial ledger). A card whose facts overlap the RF1116 holdout (10-19..12-31; the W47 card, 2026-11-20) cannot use it: the final test is the forward period after the card date (`final_test: {kind: FORWARD, from}`), terminal `FROZEN_AWAITING_FORWARD` (steps 8 and 10 `PENDING_FORWARD`, seal `AWAIT_FORWARD`); a successful holdout read for such a candidate fails C3 (`FINAL_TEST_OVERLAPS_SEEN`). A card whose facts lie before the holdout keeps the holdout path below.

## W24b: QRP-native backtester (plan-coord decision 11-23, NOTE_1 6b3be10d)

`services/research-lab-e2e/backtester.js` WRITES `LAB_REPLAY_OUTPUT_V1` from the CN_A tables the RF1116 round runner reads (dev + validation segments: `data-adapter` `buildPanel` on the frozen MINING_SPACE_V1, the DSL evaluator, `scoring.neutralizedValues` with the round's neutralisation version, the way `select` does). No external replay core, no V6 Q0 engine, no database, no network, no clock in the output.

`run({ formula, sources, rules, existingBook, candidateId, market: 'CN_A', costBook, costModelRef })` returns `{ replay, timing, diagnostics }` or `{ refusal }`. `timing` (`arm_ms`, `panel_ms`, `total_ms`, measured with hrtime) and `diagnostics` stay outside `replay`, so the replay bytes are deterministic.

Rules (frozen in the K2): `rebalance` `DAILY` or `WEEKLY` (decision day = the first trading day of the week, Monday unless it is a holiday); top `quantile` (default 0.2, at most 0.5) of the neutralised factor values of the day's pool, equal weight at entry, fills at the open of the decision day. Execution reuses holdout-scoring `longOnly`: names still in the target are kept (no drift trading), leavers are sold, newcomers bought; price limits are V-4's (`price-limits` `canBuy` / `canSell`, V6 board bands): a buy at limit-up or a sell at limit-down (or with no open price) does not execute and is retried the next day; a trade is capped at `authority.loadConfig().participationCap` of the trading day's own traded amount (`turnover_amount_cny` of that day) and the remainder is carried. `trades` lists executed trades only. Costs: registry r3 through `cost-runs.produce` on each day's trades (no rate is written in the backtester); the daily `cost` is the day's charged cost over the NAV at that day's open, `gross` the log growth of that NAV to the next open before the cost (net = gross - cost). Star defaults for plan-coord to confirm: `PORTFOLIO_WEIGHT = 0.2`, `DEFAULT_INITIAL_NAV = 1e8` CNY, 0.3% of NAV kept as cash when buying (costs are paid from cash).

Six arms in one process: baseline (same rules on the equal-weight universe, no factor), candidate, `PARAM_MINUS_20` / `PARAM_PLUS_20` (quantile x0.8 / x1.2), portfolio with and without the candidate. Every series is restricted to the dates common with the existing book. `existingBook` = `[{date, net}]` daily net returns of the existing CN_A book; `null` or empty is `BACKTEST_EXISTING_BOOK_MISSING` (never fabricated). `portfolio.without` = the book; `portfolio.with` = `(1 - w) * book + w * candidate net` (the candidate's log net converted with expm1). `portfolio.stress` = `STRESS_BOOK_WORST_20D` and `STRESS_MARKET_WORST_20D` (the market = the equal-weight baseline's gross), delta = sum of with - without over the window.

Overlap: the field `portfolio.holdings_overlap[].jaccard` keeps its name for the S4 producer's schema, but per NOTE_1 the measure is |rho| of daily net returns of the candidate against the existing portfolio: the series is the |rolling 60-day correlation| of candidate net and book net. `s4-evidence.js` now measures `overlap.holdings` and `overlap.risk` both as the full-sample |corr(candidate net, book net)| paired by date (it used the candidate's increment over the baseline for `risk` and the series mean for `holdings`); the series is validated, not averaged.

Refusals: `BACKTEST_EXISTING_BOOK_MISSING`, `BACKTEST_NO_COMMON_DATES`, `BACKTEST_TOO_FEW_DAYS`, `BACKTEST_RULES_INVALID:<rule>`, `BACKTEST_MARKET_UNSUPPORTED:<m>`, `BACKTEST_COST_BOOK_UNKNOWN:<book>`, `BACKTEST_SOURCES_INVALID` (a holdout source), `BACKTEST_FORMULA_INVALID:<code>`, `BACKTEST_NO_TRADABLE_DAYS`, and the cost-runs refusals (an item it cannot price).

`readExistingBook({ file })` reads a read-only exported series (CSV `date,net` with the header, or JSON `[{date, net}]`) and validates it: sorted, finite, no duplicate dates (`BACKTEST_BOOK_{UNREADABLE,HEADER_INVALID,EMPTY,DATE_INVALID,NOT_FINITE,DUPLICATE_DATE,NOT_SORTED}`).

## V-1 causality evidence by composition (`causality-evidence.js`)

The 12 whitelist operators were certified once with the G-CAUSAL harness (`docs/evidence/mining-operators-v1/CAUSALITY_EVIDENCE_V1.json` and its `.sha256`; all role `FEATURE`, T-C1, T-C2, T-C3 PASS). A mined DSL candidate is a composition of them, so its V-1 evidence is those records, copied unchanged, for the operators its formula uses. The harness is not re-run. Test: `tests/research_lab_causality_evidence.test.js` (composed evidence passes `runners.V1` and the K2 freeze program's V5).

- `operatorsOf(formula)`: the operator calls the formula makes, sorted. `+ - * /`, unary minus, constants and readings are not operators: they are pointwise on already certified, same-date values, are not in the evidence and carry no `CF:` id (they are not harness-tested on their own; stated here, not hidden).
- `functionIdsOf(formula)`: the `CF:` ids, `qrp:services/mining-sandbox/evaluator/operators.js#ROWS.<op>`, sorted. A formula without any operator, or with a call outside the whitelist, is refused.
- `fromExpression({ formula, k2Sha256?, dataInputs, operatorEvidence?, codeSha?, operatorsSource?, evidencePath? })` returns `{ evidence, bytes, sha256, code_sha, pinnedCodeShas, run_commit_key, v1 }`. `evidence` is a `CAUSALITY_EVIDENCE_V1` document: `contract_ref`, harness fields, seed and cut points are the operators' own; `k2_sha256` is the K2; `functions` are the used operators' records (original fields and results); `record_hash` by `causality.recordHash`; an extra `composition` block records the canonical formula, operator evidence sha256, and the DATA tokens. Same inputs give the same bytes (no clock: `registered_at` is the operator evidence's). `v1` is `{ entry: { k2, causality_evidence }, files: { causalityEvidence } }`, to be merged into the verifier input's `v1` block next to the K2 file, `runLog` and the entry's id, kind, sealed_on, k2_frozen_at.
- `dataInputs`: the PIT `DATA:` tokens the K2 carries (they are what G-PARITY reads from the K2 text; they are checked for form and recorded in `composition`; the operator records keep their original synthetic-fixture inputs).
- Refusals (`CAUSALITY_COMPOSE_*`): `OPERATOR_NOT_CERTIFIED:<op>:<NOT_IN_EVIDENCE|ROLE_NOT_FEATURE|T-C1_NOT_PASS|T-C2_NOT_PASS|T-C3_NOT_PASS>`; `OPERATOR_EVIDENCE_STALE` (operators.js changed since the evidence's commit: the existing re-run rule, plus the record's `file_sha256`); `OPERATOR_EVIDENCE_SHA_MISMATCH`; `OPERATOR_EVIDENCE_INVALID`; `NO_OPERATORS`; `K2_SHA_INVALID`; `DATA_INPUTS_MISSING` / `DATA_INPUTS_INVALID`; `CODE_SHA_UNVERIFIED`. Only the operators a formula uses are checked.

### How the AI drafter gets its CF ids, and what the freeze and V-1 need

1. Draft stage: the drafter (or the lab step that assembles the draft) calls `functionIdsOf(formula)` and writes them into `causality.functions`; the K2 freeze program renders them as `CF:` lines. It calls `fromExpression({ formula, dataInputs })` with no `k2Sha256` (the K2 is not frozen yet; V5 accepts evidence bound to an earlier K2) and gives the bytes to the program as `evidenceBytes`, `evidence_sha256 = sha256`, and `pinnedCodeShas = result.pinnedCodeShas` (`{ qrp: code_sha }`).
2. Seal/verify stage: `fromExpression({ formula, k2Sha256: <frozen K2>, dataInputs, codeSha: <qrp run commit> })` and put `qrp_run_commit` (`run_commit_key`) in the run log equal to `code_sha`. `codeSha` is accepted only when git shows `operators.js` identical at that commit; without it `code_sha` is the evidence's own commit and the run log must carry that one.
3. Not composable: when the candidate's arms are cost-gated runs (A1: roles and stages in the cost gate), V5 and G-CAUSAL demand a function with role `FILL`; the operator evidence has none, so such a K2 needs separate fill-function evidence. Signal-only arms (stage `SIGNAL`) are outside A1.


### Why pointwise arithmetic needs no harness certification (plan-coord ruling 11-23)

The operators `+`, `-`, `*`, `/` and unary minus (and numeric constants) are not in the harness evidence and carry no `CF:` id. They are pointwise: the value at (date t, code c) is a function of the operand values at the same (t, c) only. They have no window, no shift and no cross-date or cross-code access, so they cannot move information from a later date to an earlier one. Their operands are the readings (cut at their PIT availability by the panel) or the outputs of the 12 certified operators (`rank`, `zscore`, `neutralize_industry`, `neutralize_size`, `ts_mean`, `ts_std`, `ts_delta`, `ts_rank`, `ts_corr`, `sign`, `abs`, `log`), each certified with T-C1, T-C2 and T-C3. By induction on the expression tree, an expression built only from certified operators and pointwise arithmetic can use no information that one of its leaves did not already have at t, so a look-ahead would have to come from a leaf, and the leaves are certified or PIT-cut. This is a proof about the evaluator's definition of these operators (`services/mining-sandbox/evaluator/operators.js`), not a harness run; changing them to anything that is not pointwise would void it and the operator evidence re-run rule applies.

## S5 holdout mapping (3-way) and the idle existing book

S5 on the holdout is 3-way (Owner rule: insufficient is never a rejection; `holdout-outcome.js`): G-MINE PASS is S5 PASS (`CLAIM_ELIGIBLE`); G-MINE FAIL with at least the K2's `min_sample_n` holdout IC days is S5 FAIL (`STOPPED_NEGATIVE`, 不做, failure library); a FAIL short of sample or power (unknown day count included) is S5 INSUFFICIENT (`RUNNING_DESCRIBE_ONLY`) resolved through amendment section 4.2 with the host's `insufficientInputs` (without them the run waits `INSUFFICIENT_INPUTS`).

S4 evidence carries `existing_book_active_share` (share of days the existing book's net return is not zero). Below 20% the S4 body carries `limitation: EXISTING_BOOK_MOSTLY_IDLE`; the decision still rests on the marginal net contribution, and the limitation is written to the verdict's `limits` and to the feedback fact's `limits`.

## Ladder order (plan-coord NOTE_1 6b3be10d)

S2, S3 and S4 run on dev + validation, before the holdout: the round's selection, the backtester's replay output (step 7, `replay_output.json`, written inside QRP from the tables the round read) and the frozen K2 feed `dev-validation.js`. The verifier V-1..V-6 gates the move to S5 (it runs when S2..S4 all PASS; a candidate that stops earlier gets the engine's verdict, step 8 `NOT_APPLICABLE`). S5 is the holdout (holdout-path candidates, from 2027-01-01, after the exempt scorer's read; evidence: the G-MINE record's pass) or the forward test (review-derived candidates). Terminal `FROZEN_AWAITING_HOLDOUT` / `FROZEN_AWAITING_FORWARD` means S0 to S4 and V-1..V-6 are done and only S5 is pending (step 10 `PENDING_*`). The V-1 evidence is composed from the certified operators (`causality-evidence.js`), V-3 statistics come from the validation folds (`v3-statistics.js fromSelection`, DSR through `holdout-scoring.dsrFromValidation`).

S4 overlap threshold `max_overlap` = 0.7 (★ default, plan-coord NOTE_1; equal to `factor-library` `DEDUPE.RHO_MAX`): |rho| of the candidate's daily net return against the existing CN_A book's, strictly greater refuses. It belongs in `config/promotion_terms_star.v1.json` (QRP #150) with source NOTE_1 `6b3be10d`; that file is not part of this PR.
